How are these recommendations calculated?

Match = 65% skill alignment + 30% interest alignment + 5% subject relevance. For each required skill, alignment is your rating divided by the illustrative target, capped at 100%; these values are averaged. Interest alignment averages relevant interest ratings out of five. Subject relevance is 100% for a listed related subject and 50% otherwise, recognising that career changes are possible. Education informs entry-route guidance and does not change the score. Ties follow catalogue order.

Role profiles and targets are manually authored examples, not measured occupational standards. This version uses a transparent rule-based method, not a trained machine-learning model.
